# Compliance: regulations

## Regulatory Quick Reference

### GDPR Core Requirements
- Lawful basis for each processing activity (Article 6)
- Purpose limitation (Article 5(1)(b))
- Data minimization (Article 5(1)(c))
- Storage limitation (Article 5(1)(e))
- Integrity and confidentiality (Article 5(1)(f))
- Accountability - be able to demonstrate compliance (Article 5(2))
- DPIA for high-risk processing (Article 35)
- Privacy by design and by default (Article 25)
- Processor agreements with defined terms (Article 28)
- International transfer safeguards including SCCs
- Rights facilitation within one month (Articles 12–22)

### CCPA/CPRA Framework
- Right to know, delete, correct, opt out of sale/sharing, limit sensitive PI use
- Notice-at-collection before or at collection
- Service-provider/contractor agreements with purpose limitations
- CPPA rules on dark patterns and ADMT notices
- 45-day response window for rights requests
- Sensitive PI requires explicit purpose controls and user limitation handling

### LGPD (Brazil)
- Lawful bases (Articles 6, 7) - similar structure to GDPR
- Data subject rights including access, correction, deletion, portability
- Accountability and governance obligations
- Transfer governance for international data flows

### HIPAA/HITECH
- Security Rule: administrative, physical, technical safeguards for ePHI
- BAA requirements for all business associates handling PHI
- HITECH breach notification for unsecured PHI
- Minimum necessary standard for PHI access and disclosure
- Audit controls and access logging

### PCI DSS
- Never store CVV/CVC after authorization
- Encrypt cardholder data at rest and in transit
- Restrict access on need-to-know with monitoring
- Scope CDE tightly - systems that could affect CDE security are in scope
- Strong cryptography for open-network transmission
- Track and monitor all access to cardholder data

### SOC 2 / SOX
- SOC 2: controls for security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, privacy
- SOX: internal control over financial reporting - change control, access control, evidence
- Preserved evidence, approval workflows, periodic access review, defensible procedures

### EU AI Act Timeline
- **Already in force**: AI literacy duties, prohibited practices
- **Since August 2025**: GPAI obligations
- **From August 2026**: Article 50 transparency duties, majority of AI Act rules
- **Later timing**: certain high-risk systems embedded in regulated products
