# DNN Manager 1.7.1

**Cards and edges you can drag.** In the Default density the sidebar, the page and the panel are rounded cards, as in VS Code, and you can drag the edges between them - the sidebar's too - to resize, close and open them.

## Highlights

### The window as cards
- In the **Default** density the sidebar, the page and the panel are rounded cards with a 5-pixel gap between them. The title bar and the status bar are one surface with them, without lines.
- The title bar is 30 pixels high and the status bar 24 in both densities, so the cards get that room.
- **Compact** keeps them flush, divided by 1-pixel lines. The thicker bar above the panel is gone.

### Drag every edge
- Drag the sidebar's edge to make it wider or narrower, from 160 to 600 pixels. Its width is kept between starts, and the reset button in **Customize Layout** puts it back.
- Where the sidebar's edge meets the panel's, drag both at once.
- Three dots show where an edge can be dragged (in **Compact**, the lines between the parts), and the edge turns blue while you drag it.
- Drag the sidebar or the panel down to nothing and it closes; drag it out again from its edge - the window's side, or the page's bottom for the panel - and it opens.
- Let go smaller than its smallest size (160 pixels for the sidebar, 90 for the panel) and it slides out to it; under half of that, it slides closed.

### Sliding open and closed
- The sidebar and the panel slide open and closed from the title bar's buttons, **Ctrl+B** and **Ctrl+J**, the panel's **✕** and **Customize Layout**. Pressed again while they slide, they turn back.
- The panel's **✕** now puts the keyboard back on the page, like **Ctrl+J**.

### Customize Layout
- **Menu Bar** shows or hides the app's name in the title bar - also **Show or hide the menu bar** in the command palette.
- Rounder icons. **Default** and **Compact** show the sidebar, the page and the panel spaced apart or packed together, and the **Customize Layout** button in the title bar shows the Default one.

### A tidier status bar
- Under the icons-only or hidden sidebar, the IIS cell keeps its state and its buttons, as wide as what it shows, instead of a dot with a **⋮** menu.
- RAM, CPU and Disk are evenly spaced, and the IIS buttons are smaller, with a gap before them.

## Other changes
- The sidebar's entries and the Settings categories have less room on the left and right, and more between the icon and the name. In the sidebar, the first entry and **Settings** are as far from the top and bottom as from the sides.
- The **Columns** button above the Projects table has a rounded icon.
- DNN Manager looks for a newer release every hour; 1.7.0 checked every six hours.
- Fixed: the text in the status bar and the title bar sat below the middle, beside icons that are centred.

## Upgrading
Install over 1.7.0 as usual. Your settings keep their format; `layout.menuBarVisible` is added with its default (`true`).
- The title bar and the status bar are lower in the Default density too.
- Under a narrow or hidden sidebar, the IIS cell no longer has a **⋮** menu - its buttons are always there.

## Tested
- All 112 fast automated tests pass, as in 1.7.0. The layout settings test now also covers the menu bar setting.
